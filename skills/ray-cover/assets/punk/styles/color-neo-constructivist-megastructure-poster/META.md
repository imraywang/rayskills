style_anchors:
  - neo-constructivist poster composition with monumental scale
  - enormous cropped subject occupying 40-80 percent of the frame
  - sharp diagonal cuts, arcs, wedges, rays, tracks, and high-tension geometry
  - bold unexpected 3-4 color palette with uneven color area distribution
  - restrained but powerful title integrated into the visual structure
  - vintage screen-print texture, halftone dots, paper grain, and slight ink misregistration
cover_shape_adaptation:
  - main subject should feel too large for the frame, often cropped by edges
  - title should behave as structure, pressure, direction, or architectural mass
  - geometry should organize scale, rhythm, direction, and spatial dominance
  - wide covers may use strong left-right collision, diagonal flow, or monumental panorama
must_preserve:
  - one core subject, one visual center, one motion direction, and one metaphor
  - strong first-glance impact and clear subject recognition
  - 3-4 main colors only, with a dominant color and concentrated accent color
  - minimal readable text with total text area under 15 percent
avoid_when_applying_to_cover:
  - decorative geometry with no spatial function
  - average-size subject placed normally in the frame
  - safe conventional industry palettes or repeated yellow-background formulas
  - crowded text, long explanations, or multiple competing focal points

## Style Intent

以 20 世纪构成主义为视觉骨架，融合彩色限色系统、现代编辑设计和复古印刷质感，制造巨构、压迫、震撼和强烈方向动势。该 style 只负责新构成主义巨构海报语言、极端尺度关系、几何切割、限色系统和文字结构；平台适配、长文提炼和通用封面约束由 `punk-cover` 负责。

## Use For

- 热点事件、体育赛事、产品发布、城市建筑、科技观点和强传播封面
- 需要第一眼震撼、巨大主体和强几何张力的内容
- 活动海报、社交媒体封面、视频封面和文章头图

## Avoid

- 普通主体配背景图
- 多主题、多中心或元素堆叠
- 甜美、柔和、低对比或装饰性拼贴
- 长段说明文字、复杂信息图或安全行业配色
